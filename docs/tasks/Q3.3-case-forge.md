## Scenario walked through

- **Persona and record:** Karolina Wiśniewska in the employer workspace; Marek Nowak's seeded AI Product Manager application at BrightLabs.
- **Criterion statuses:** problem and pilot — evidence; method of checking — evidence; personal contribution and tools — follow-up.
- **Next step:** ask about the error threshold and the candidate's role in setting it.
- **Evidence entered:** “Mierzyłbym czas sprawdzenia i liczbę poprawek; każdy niepewny wynik zatwierdzałaby osoba z finansów.” The note identifies this as a measurable outcome with human review, while the answer does not name a stop threshold.
- **Open question entered:** “Jaki próg błędów zatrzymałby pilota i jaką rolę miałby Pan w ustaleniu tego progu?”
- **Observed result:** the session summary showed all three statuses, the selected next step, evidence and question. Application stage remained “Aplikacja wysłana”. The exercise is session-only and no data was sent or persisted.
- **Answer provenance:** the work-sample response is fictional, written for the prototype and not generated by AI or submitted by Marek. It contains the fictional claim that ChatGPT helped list exceptions. The pre-existing 72% Match Score and summary are prepared examples; they stayed visible but were not used as evidence for the choice. No live AI service was called.
- **Limit:** this was an internal walkthrough of the prototype, not a session with a real recruiter or evidence that the sample measures candidate skill.
